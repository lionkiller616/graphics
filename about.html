Our Story

Founded in 2018, Graphics Design System began as a small collection of UI components for internal projects. As our team grew, we recognized the need for a more systematic approach to design that could scale across multiple products and platforms.

Today, our design system powers hundreds of applications used by millions of people worldwide, helping teams maintain consistency while accelerating their design and development workflows.
