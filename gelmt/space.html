The Art of Space

Breathing Room in Design

What is Space in Design?

Space refers to the area around, between, or within elements of a design. It can be:

  • Positive space: The occupied areas (objects, text, images)
  • Negative space: The empty areas around and between objects
  • Active space: Areas that draw attention and create movement
  • Passive space: Areas that provide rest and organization
  • Micro space: Small gaps between elements (letter spacing, padding)
  • Macro space: Large empty areas (margins, backgrounds)

Types of Space

The main subjects or focal points

Micro Space

Macro Space

Large empty areas in layout

Psychology of Space

Space affects how we perceive and interact with designs:

  • Cluttered space feels chaotic, overwhelming, or energetic
  • Open space conveys luxury, calm, or importance
  • Balanced space appears harmonious and professional
  • Asymmetrical space creates dynamic tension and interest
  • Negative space can form hidden shapes and meanings
