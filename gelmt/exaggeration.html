Exaggeration

Pushing Reality for Impact

What is Exaggeration?

Exaggeration in animation involves pushing the boundaries of reality to enhance expressiveness and clarity. It's not about making things look wrong, but about amplifying emotions, actions, and poses to make them more impactful and entertaining for the audience.

This principle is particularly powerful in cartoony animation, where characters can stretch, squash, and distort in ways that would be impossible in reality. However, subtle exaggeration can also be used in more realistic animation to underscore a character's feeling or the force of an action. The key is to remain true to the established style of the animation.

Why Exaggeration Works

Exaggeration plays on our natural tendency to notice and react to heightened stimuli. By amplifying visual or temporal cues, animators can ensure that important information or emotional states are instantly recognizable and leave a stronger impression.
