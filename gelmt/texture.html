Visual and Physical Surface Quality

What is Texture in Design?

Texture refers to the surface quality of a design element, which can be perceived both visually and physically. Texture can:

  • Add depth and dimension to flat designs
  • Create visual interest and contrast
  • Evoke emotional responses through tactile associations
  • Improve usability by providing visual cues
  • Enhance realism in representational designs

Psychology of Texture

Textures evoke subconscious associations:

  • Smooth textures feel modern, sleek, and efficient
  • Rough textures suggest authenticity, age, and naturalness
  • Soft textures convey comfort, warmth, and approachability
  • Hard textures communicate strength, durability, and coldness
  • Patterned textures can create rhythm, movement, or cultural associations
