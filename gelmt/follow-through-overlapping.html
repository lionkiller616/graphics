Follow Through & Overlapping Action

Creating Natural Movement

What Are These Principles?

trending_flat Follow Through

Follow Through is the principle that dictates that parts of a body or object continue to move after the main body has stopped. Think of a character's hair or clothing settling after they come to a halt, or the way a tail swings to a stop after a dog sits down.

layers Overlapping Action

Overlapping Action is the principle that different parts of a body or object move at different speeds or at different times. This means that not everything starts and stops moving simultaneously. For example, a character's hand might start reaching before their body turns, or their head might lead a walk cycle slightly.

Psychology Behind Follow Through & Overlapping

These principles make animation feel more natural and believable because they accurately reflect the laws of physics in our world. Objects have inertia – they resist changes in motion. When a character stops, their loosely connected parts (hair, clothes, limbs) continue moving due to this inertia before settling.
