Scale and Proportion in Design

What is Size in Design?

Size refers to the dimensions of elements in relation to each other and to the overall composition. Size can be:

  • Absolute: Measured in specific units (pixels, inches)
  • Relative: Compared to other elements in the design
  • Hierarchical: Used to show importance and organization
  • Proportional: Maintaining consistent ratios between elements
  • Exaggerated: Purposefully distorted for effect

Dividing space into thirds for balance

Psychology of Size

Size affects how we perceive and interact with elements:

  • Large elements feel important, powerful, or dominant
  • Small elements appear delicate, precise, or less significant
  • Unexpected scale creates surprise and grabs attention
  • Consistent sizing suggests order and professionalism
  • Varied sizing adds dynamism and visual interest
