Solid Drawing

The Foundation of Believable Animation

What is Solid Drawing?

Solid Drawing is the principle of rendering animated forms in three-dimensional space, giving them volume, weight, and balance. It's about understanding anatomy, perspective, and how shapes exist and interact in a 3D environment, even when drawn on a 2D surface.

Mastering solid drawing allows animators to create characters and objects that feel grounded and believable. It ensures that characters maintain their form and volume from any angle, contributing significantly to the overall realism and appeal of the animation.

Why Solid Drawing Matters

Our visual perception is fundamentally based on processing information from a three-dimensional world. Solid drawing principles align with how we naturally perceive objects and movement in reality.
