The Dimension of Form

Three-Dimensional Expression in Design

What is Form in Design?

Form refers to three-dimensional objects or the illusion of three dimensions in two-dimensional design. Form can be:

  • Geometric: Precise and mathematical (cubes, spheres)
  • Organic: Natural and irregular (rocks, plants, animals)
  • Abstract: Simplified or exaggerated representations
  • Realistic: Accurate depictions of real-world objects
  • Implied: Suggested through shading and perspective

Types of Form

Geometric Forms

Precise, mathematical shapes with clear edges

Organic Forms

Irregular, natural shapes found in nature

Abstract Forms

Simplified or exaggerated representations

Open Forms

Forms that interact with surrounding space

Closed Forms

Self-contained forms with clear boundaries
