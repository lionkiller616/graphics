Squash and Stretch

The Fundamental Principle of Animation That Brings Life to Objects

What is Squash and Stretch?

Squash and Stretch is the animation principle that gives objects the illusion of weight, volume, and flexibility. It's the foundation that makes animated movement feel believable and alive, whether it's a bouncing ball or a character's facial expression.

When an object moves, its shape changes in response to forces acting upon it. A ball squashes when it hits the ground and stretches when it's in motion. This deformation, while maintaining consistent volume, creates the illusion of physics in motion.

The Psychology Behind Squash and Stretch

Our brains are wired to understand physical properties through movement. Squash and stretch works because it aligns with our subconscious understanding of physics and material properties.

psychology

Perception of Weight

The amount of squash tells our brain how heavy an object is. More squash suggests more mass and impact force.

momentum

Sense of Motion

Stretching in the direction of movement enhances the perception of speed and momentum.

touch_app

Tactile Feedback

Deformation provides visual feedback about the material's properties - rubbery, stiff, soft, etc.
