What is Staging?

Staging is the principle of presenting an idea so that it is completely and unmistakably clear to the audience. It involves arranging elements within the "frame" or scene to direct the viewer's eye to what is important and to ensure the narrative is easy to follow.

Effective staging eliminates confusion and focuses attention. It can be achieved through various techniques such as character positioning, background design, lighting, camera angles, and even the timing of actions.

The Psychology Behind Staging

Effective staging works because it aligns with fundamental principles of visual perception and cognitive processing. Our brains prioritize information based on factors like contrast, movement, size, and position.
