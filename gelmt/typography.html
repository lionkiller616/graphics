Visual Language of Text

What is Typography in Design?

Typography is the art and technique of arranging type to make written language legible, readable, and appealing. It involves:

  • Selecting typefaces, point sizes, line lengths
  • Adjusting spacing between letters and lines
  • Creating hierarchy and contrast
  • Ensuring readability and accessibility
  • Conveying tone and personality

Kerning

Space between individual letter pairs

Leading

Vertical space between lines of text

Tracking

Uniform spacing between all letters

Hierarchy

Visual organization of text elements

Psychology of Typography

Typography significantly affects perception and emotion:

  • Serif fonts convey tradition, reliability, and respectability
  • Sans-serif fonts feel modern, clean, and approachable
  • Script fonts suggest elegance, creativity, or femininity
  • Display fonts create strong personality and impact
  • Bold weight communicates importance and strength
  • Light weight appears delicate, refined, or elegant
