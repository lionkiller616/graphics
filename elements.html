Line

Psychology of Lines

  • Horizontal lines: Calm, stability, tranquility
  • Vertical lines: Strength, power, growth
  • Diagonal lines: Movement, action, dynamism
  • Curved lines: Fluidity, softness, comfort
  • Zigzag lines: Excitement, tension, energy

Shape

Psychology of Shapes

  • Circles: Unity, protection, infinity, community
  • Squares/Rectangles: Stability, reliability, order, formality
  • Triangles: Direction, purpose, energy, conflict
  • Organic shapes: Natural, comforting, approachable, fluid
  • Geometric shapes: Structured, efficient, man-made, precise

Psychology of Forms

  • Cubes: Stability, honesty, containment, structure
  • Spheres: Unity, completeness, harmony, movement
  • Pyramids: Hierarchy, stability, mystery, power
  • Cylinders: Utility, stability combined with movement
  • Organic forms: Naturalness, complexity, softness

Texture

Psychology of Texture

  • Smooth textures: Sleek, modern, efficient, clean
  • Rough textures: Natural, rugged, aged, rustic
  • Soft textures: Comfort, warmth, approachability, luxury
  • Hard textures: Strength, durability, coldness, structure

Space

Subject
Negative Space (Whitespace)

Psychology of Space

  • Positive space: Contains main subject, demands attention, defines focus.
  • Negative space: Provides breathing room, enhances comprehension, creates balance.
  • Whitespace: Creates elegance, sophistication, clarity, minimalism.
  • Crowded space: Generates energy, intensity, chaos, urgency.
  • Balanced space: Promotes harmony, stability, professionalism, order.

Imagery

Psychology of Imagery

  • Icons: Quick recognition, universal understanding, simplicity.
  • Illustrations: Personality, creativity, storytelling, uniqueness.
  • Abstract images: Evoke emotion, interpretation, modernity, intrigue.
  • Photorealistic images: Build trust, authenticity, relatability, clarity.
  • Stylized images: Create brand identity, memorability, specific mood.

Psychology of Typography

  • Serif fonts: Tradition, reliability, respectability, formality.
  • Sans-serif fonts: Modern, clean, approachable, objective.
  • Script fonts: Elegance, creativity, femininity, personality.
  • Display fonts: Impact, personality, uniqueness, attention-grabbing.
  • Monospace fonts: Technical, precise, retro, code-like.
